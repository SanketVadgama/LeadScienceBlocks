What you can build with these blocks

These are not full websites — they are modular pieces. Mix and match by industry or by pattern.

  • Industry microsite — Pick one industry (e.g. Construction) and stack Hero + Projects + Services + Crew + Contact for a full landing page.
  • Agency portfolio — Use Featured Projects + From the sites + Partners-style cards to show case studies across clients.
  • Clinic / practice site — Specialties + Care Team + Real Results + a contact form for a medical or dental practice.
  • Law firm brochure site — Practice Areas + Partners + a simple inquiry form.
  • Spa booking funnel — Soft/slow rituals + Eight rooms + Let’s Build–style form for appointments.
  • Internal design system — Keep this showcase page as the “source of truth” for approved sections your team can copy into client projects.
  • Hybrid pages — e.g. Construction Hero + Medical Care Team pattern + Wellness gallery for a multi-brand holding company site.

How to reuse: open the matching folder → copy the three files (or two) for that section into your project → link the CSS/JS in your page. Change images, text, and colors as needed.
